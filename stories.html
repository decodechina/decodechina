项目行程Program Itinerary

June 15-21, 2026 | Beijing2026年6月15-21日 | 北京

无论你正在打造产品、投资前沿科技、制定企业战略，还是准备迈向领导岗位，理解中国科技生态，已经不再是可选项。

一场以北京中关村为核心坐标、深入中国 AI、机器人、先进制造与创新生态的 7 天沉浸式体验。 Whether you are building products, investing in frontier tech, shaping corporate strategy, or preparing for leadership roles, understanding China's tech ecosystem is no longer optional.

A curated deep dive into China’s AI, robotics, manufacturing, and innovation ecosystem — centered in Beijing’s Zhongguancun, the birthplace of China’s modern technology industry.

DAY 1

抵达北京 · 开营交流 Arrival & Opening Session

抵达北京，入住中关村核心区域酒店。

晚间举办开营晚宴与主题交流：
《2026：中国科技竞争力的真相》

与来自科技、投资、学术等领域的嘉宾及同行展开交流。 Arrive in Beijing and check into the Zhongguancun core area.

Evening opening dinner and keynote discussion:
“Understanding China’s Technology Landscape in 2026”

Meet fellow participants, industry practitioners, and invited guests from academia, investment, and technology sectors.

DAY 2

算力革命与芯片生态 Computing Infrastructure & Semiconductor Ecosystem

深入中国自研 GPU 企业，了解：
• AI 算力基础设施
• GPU 技术路线
• 大模型推理生态

观察全球供应链与智能制造系统如何协同运作。

晚间数字生活体验：体验北京移动支付与数字人民币生态。 Visit one of China’s leading domestic GPU companies and explore:
• AI computing infrastructure
• GPU development
• large model inference systems

Understand how China’s global supply chain and smart manufacturing systems operate at scale.

Evening Digital Experience: Experience Beijing’s mobile-first urban ecosystem through digital RMB, mobile payments, and smart city infrastructure.

📍 摩尔线程 GPU 实验室 / 联想未来中心 Moore Threads GPU Lab / Lenovo Future Center
DAY 3

AI 大模型与自动驾驶 AI & Autonomous Mobility

与研究员交流中国大模型与开源 AI 生态。

体验 L4 级 Robotaxi 自动驾驶与智能交通系统。

AI 沙龙交流：与 AI 创业者、研究员及投资人进行深度交流。 Engage with researchers and explore large language models, open-source AI ecosystems, and China’s AI research landscape.

Experience L4 autonomous Robotaxi demonstrations and intelligent transportation systems in real urban environments.

Evening AI Salon: Private networking session with local AI founders, developers, researchers, and investors.

📍 北京智源人工智能研究院（BAAI）/ 百度 Apollo Park BAAI / Baidu Apollo Park
DAY 4

顶级学术与创新生态 Universities, Innovation Policy & Ecosystem Development

围绕中国创新模式、产业政策与全球科技竞争展开闭门交流。

系统理解中国科技产业 40 年演进历程：从联想、新浪到 AI、机器人与前沿创新企业。 Closed-door discussions on China’s innovation model, industrial policy, and technology & globalization.

Trace the evolution of China’s technology ecosystem: from Lenovo and Sina to AI, robotics, and frontier innovation companies.

📍 清华大学苏世民书院 / 中关村展示中心 Schwarzman College, Tsinghua University / ZGC Exhibition Center
DAY 5

生命科学与具身智能 Biotechnology & Embodied AI

了解 AI 如何推动生物医药、新药发现与生命科学创新。

现场观摩具身智能与机器人视觉大模型在真实、非结构化环境中的运作与演示。 Explore how AI is accelerating biotechnology, drug discovery, and life science innovation.

Observe embodied AI and robotic vision systems operating in real-world, unstructured environments.

📍 百济神州 / 中关村生命科学园 / 银河通用机器人 BeiGene / Changping Life Science Park / Galbot Robotics
DAY 6

先进制造与物流自动化 Advanced Manufacturing & Logistics Automation

近距离观察“黑灯工厂”与高度自动化新能源汽车生产体系。每 76 秒产出一辆 SU7 的智能制造奇迹。

理解 AI 与机器人如何支撑大规模物流网络。

结业晚宴与证书颁发：项目总结、交流与官方证书授予。 Witness one of China’s most advanced “dark factories,” where Xiaomi SU7 vehicles roll off the production line in as little as 76 seconds.

Explore large-scale automation systems powering one of the world’s most sophisticated logistics networks.

Closing Dinner & Certificate Ceremony: Program wrap-up, reflection, and networking dinner.

📍 小米汽车超级工厂 / 京东物流中心 Xiaomi EV Factory / JD.com Logistics Center
DAY 7

文化体验与返程 Cultural Exploration & Departure

参观故宫、天坛等北京文化地标。

随后送机返程。 Visit iconic cultural landmarks such as the Forbidden City and the Temple of Heaven.

Airport transfer and departure.
